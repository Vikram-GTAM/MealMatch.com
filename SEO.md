# Meal Match SEO plan

## Search intent and keyword focus

These are initial targets based on the current product and Hyderabad service
area, not claims about search volume or ranking difficulty. Validate and refine
them with Google Search Console and Keyword Planner once the site has traffic.

| Priority | Keyword theme | Search intent | Recommended page use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | personalised meal plans in Hyderabad | Find a tailored meal plan locally | Homepage title, description, hero copy |
| Secondary | healthy meal plans Hyderabad | Compare healthy local meal options | Homepage headings and plan copy |
| Secondary | high-protein meals Hyderabad | Find meals supporting nutrition goals | Menu and nutrition sections |
| Secondary | meal delivery Hyderabad | Find prepared meals delivered locally | Use only where delivery coverage and ordering details are explicit |
| Supporting | vegetarian meal plans Hyderabad | Find vegetarian options | Add a dedicated page only if the menu and ordering flow support it |

Use natural variations in helpful copy; do not repeat exact-match phrases
unnaturally or add a `meta keywords` tag.

## On-site work completed

- Localised the document title, description, social-sharing metadata, and hero
  copy for Hyderabad and personalised meal-plan intent.
- Added `Organization` and `WebSite` structured data using the public site URL,
  existing Instagram profile, and Hyderabad service area. No street address,
  phone number, opening hours, or ratings are asserted because those details
  are not currently published in the site.
- Kept the canonical URL and sitemap focused on the sole public page. Add URLs
  to the sitemap when indexable pages are launched.

## Off-site priorities

These actions need access to the business's external accounts and/or verified
business details, so they are recommendations rather than changes made by this
repository:

1. Claim and complete a Google Business Profile if the business is eligible.
   Use the real service area, consistent business name/contact details, and
   only categories and delivery areas that accurately describe the operation.
2. Keep the existing Instagram profile complete and link it to the website.
   Publish original menu, preparation, and nutrition content that points
   interested customers to relevant pages.
3. Request honest reviews from actual customers through permitted channels;
   never offer incentives for positive reviews or publish fabricated reviews.
4. Seek relevant local mentions from Hyderabad food, fitness, and community
   publishers through genuine partnerships or useful editorial contributions.
   Avoid paid link schemes and bulk directory submissions.
5. Track referrals and monitor branded/local queries in Search Console and
   analytics. Review the results monthly before expanding keyword targets.

Before implementing more local listings or delivery claims, publish verified
service coverage and a consistent customer contact method on the website.
